Evgeny’s note · October 2026 · data to May 2026

Middle East crude output fell from 28.7 mb/d in February to 15.8 mb/d in May 2026. The reservoirs did not change; the export routes did. Producers with a pipeline around Hormuz kept most of their output: Saudi Arabia (East-West line to Yanbu) about 62% of its February level, the UAE (Habshan–Fujairah) about 65%. Those without a bypass kept far less: Iraq about 30%, Kuwait about 20%, Qatar and Bahrain under 20%. Oman, which loads outside the Strait, produced more than before.

For FEL work this is the point of the exercise: nameplate capacity is not exportable production, and the route to market belongs in the concept, not in the risk register.
