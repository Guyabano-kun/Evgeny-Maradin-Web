Water Injection Re-engineering

Challenged a technically valid but commercially disproportionate end-of-life redevelopment concept and developed an alternative.

ClientSalym Petroleum Development (Shell JV)
Period2023–2024
RoleConcept lead
GateDecision Review Board
80%80% CAPEX reduction ($3.6M saved) and a 300% increase in Profitability Index. Approved at Decision Review Board.

Context

An existing water injection system approaching end-of-life, with a significant CAPEX requirement under the original redevelopment concept.

Role

Concept lead. Initiated the re-evaluation of the original approach.

Challenge

The original concept was technically valid but commercially disproportionate given the remaining asset life and production profile.

Action

  • Challenged the original concept.
  • Developed an alternative engineering approach.
  • Structured the business case and prepared the DRB submission.

Result

80% CAPEX reduction ($3.6M saved). Profitability Index increased by 300%. Approved at Decision Review Board.
