Field-Wide Network Models Behind the Studies

Validated, extended and used pad-level Petex GAP models of the oil gathering and water injection systems, around 100 pads and more than 1,500 wells, as the hydraulic basis for five of the studies on this site.

ClientSalym Petroleum Development (Shell JV until 2023)
Period2017–2024
RoleValidated and extended the models
ToolPetex GAP
1,500+Around 100 pads and more than 1,500 wells in the gathering and water injection models that carried the hydraulics in five studies from 2017 to 2024.

Context

A mature multi-field asset with a large oil gathering network and a water injection system built as two independent high-pressure systems. Field-wide network models of both existed in Petex GAP, built pad by pad, around 100 pads, because well-by-well versions of more than 1,500 wells ran too slowly for study work.

Role

The base models were built by others. I validated them before each forward-looking study, extended them to the prospective development areas and used them as the hydraulic basis for concept and project work.

Challenge

A network model is only as good as its last check. Before a study looks years ahead, the model has to match the network as it runs today, or every option inherits the same error.

Action

  • Validated the gathering and water injection models before each forward-looking study.
  • Extended both models to the prospective development areas, adding the planned pads and pipelines.
  • Used the pad-level models as the calculation basis for development, debottlenecking and infrastructure studies across the asset.

Result

The models carried the hydraulics in five studies on this site: Portfolio Integration – 6 Fields, Water Injection Re-engineering, South Hub – Brownfield Expansion, Minor CAPEX Portfolio, Well-Pad & Corridor Siting Method.
