Field-Wide Network Models: Gathering and Water Injection

Built and ran pad-level Petex GAP models of the oil gathering and water injection systems, around 100 pads and more than 1,500 wells, used as the calculation basis across the asset's projects.

ClientSalym Petroleum Development (Shell JV until 2023)
Period2017–2024
RoleSenior Concept Engineer, then Concept Development Manager
ToolPetex GAP
1,500+Around 100 pads and more than 1,500 wells in field-wide gathering and water injection models: the hydraulic basis for the asset's projects from 2017 to 2024.

Context

A mature multi-field asset with a large oil gathering network and a water injection system built as two independent high-pressure systems. Concept, debottlenecking and development decisions needed a hydraulic basis covering the whole network, not one pipeline at a time.

Role

Built and maintained the field-wide network models and used them as the hydraulic basis for concept and project studies.

Challenge

Well-by-well models of more than 1,500 wells were too slow to run and maintain for study work. The models had to stay fast enough for option screening while keeping the network detail that sets pressures and capacities.

Action

  • Built the oil gathering and water injection network models in Petex GAP.
  • Modelled both systems pad by pad, around 100 pads, instead of well by well, which kept run times practical for scenario work.
  • Modelled water injection as two independent high-pressure systems.
  • Used the models as the common calculation basis for projects across the asset: development scenarios, debottlenecking and infrastructure changes.

Result

The models were the calculation basis for the asset's projects from 2017 to 2024. Alexander Galkin, who managed Evgeny directly, wrote that the field-wide pipeline network model, more than 500 km, “proved critical for evaluating alternative development scenarios and significantly contributed to the company’s field development plan update.”
