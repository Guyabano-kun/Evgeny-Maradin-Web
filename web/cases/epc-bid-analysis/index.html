EPC Bid Analysis & Contract Strategy

Decomposed bid escalation drivers across a major upstream portfolio and structured scenarios around schedule, FEED maturity, scope and risk allocation.

ClientNational oil company, MENA
Period2026
RoleIndependent advisory
ScopeSeparation & water injection packages
$6B+Scenario and decision-support framework adopted by management as the basis for contract renegotiation.

Context

A national oil company with several major EPC packages (separation and water injection facilities) where bids came in significantly above approved estimates. Tenders were cancelled under management pressure.

Role

Independent advisory: analytical support on contracting strategy and scenario development.

Challenge

The cost estimate was approved years before tender. Market escalation, an outdated FEED and aggressive schedule and LD terms combined into a risk premium that contractors priced fully upfront under LSTK.

Action

  • Decomposed the bid-to-estimate gap into controllable and non-controllable components.
  • Quantified risk exposure per package.
  • Built a scenario framework: renegotiation (schedule extension and LD reduction), selective BOOT restructuring for long-life assets, phased scope.
  • Designed a pre-tender gate KPI set: Schedule Confidence Index, TCE Age Index, Interface Register Completeness.

Result

The framework was presented to and adopted by management as the basis for contract renegotiation. Renegotiations were initiated.
