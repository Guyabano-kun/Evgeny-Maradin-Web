Brownfield Programme – South Hub

Brought subsurface, facilities and operations onto one set of assumptions and one set of selection criteria before FEED.

ClientSalym Petroleum Development (Shell JV)
AssetSouth Hub, Salym cluster
RoleStudy Manager
PhaseFEL1 – Pre-FEED
GateDecision-ready concept approved at gate review. Scope uncertainty reduced before FEED entry.

Context

A mature producing cluster with declining efficiency, increasing operational constraints and ageing infrastructure.

Role

Study Manager (FEL1 – Pre-FEED).

Challenge

Subsurface, surface facilities and operations teams were working from different assumptions. The concept was not aligned with execution realities.

Action

  • Structured development options across disciplines.
  • Facilitated cross-functional alignment on assumptions.
  • Defined concept selection criteria combining technical, cost and operational constraints.

Result

Decision-ready concept approved at gate review. Scope uncertainty reduced before FEED entry and downstream rework minimised.
