Portfolio Integration – 6 Fields

Restructured development across six mature assets and identified shared infrastructure that changed the portfolio investment case.

ClientSalym Petroleum Development (Shell JV)
Period2017–2023
RoleConcept development lead
PhaseFEL1 – Pre-FEED
~57%~57% portfolio CAPEX reduction (≈ $48M). Investment recommendation approved at shareholder level.

Context

Multiple mature assets with fragmented, duplicated infrastructure and high combined CAPEX.

Role

Led concept development and the cross-field integration study (FEL1 – Pre-FEED).

Challenge

Each asset was planned independently, with no visibility of cross-field synergies. Individual CAPEX estimates were commercially unviable as a portfolio.

Action

  • Restructured the development approach across six fields.
  • Identified infrastructure sharing opportunities between assets.
  • Prepared the integrated investment case for the JV shareholders.

Result

~57% CAPEX reduction (≈ $48M). The investment recommendation was approved at shareholder level.
